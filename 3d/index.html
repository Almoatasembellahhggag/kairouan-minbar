Numbers are those of the panel site: Saladin's, placed by the saved edits (N is a new panel, ? a place with no panel assigned), or Golvin's. They run over the side and the outside of the mihrab-side rail; the inside of that rail has the same number with r. The maqsūra-side rail, which neither of them numbered, is q1 to q18 from the top, with r on its inside. Stairs: riser.panel.

What this is built from

The mihrab-side face is Creswell's photograph (1940, Pl. 89a) laid flat and scaled to his length of 3.93 m; that gives a height of 3.35 m against his 3.31 m. Panel outlines are the ones traced on that photograph for the panel site, and the numbers are Saladin's (1899, fig. 52).

The stair has eleven steps, one over each of columns 3 to 13; their rise follows the slope of the stair string. Riser panels are counted from the fonds Poinssot photographs taken after 1910 (scans 013, 015, 018).

The reconstruction drawings are the ones on the panel site, drawn from photographs and not measured. Each is fitted to the traced outline of its panel, so its proportions follow the panel on Creswell's photograph and can differ a little from the drawing itself. The drawing of panel 50 stands where that panel was before 1907. The drawing of rail panel 66 is left out: that panel is not on the rail after 1907.

Compare with the old photo puts Garrigues's photograph of before 1907 beside the model. Each pair of details is straightened to the same shape, so a panel can be checked against any place on the model. Where a panel is now is read from the edits saved on the panel site, in this browser if there are any; nothing here changes them.

Estimated, not measured: the width (1.0 m), the height of the seat, the backrest and the posts. The maqsūra-side face is left blank.

Rail photos puts a photograph on every rail panel, cut from the six photographs of the new rails laid flat during the restoration (Bibliothèque de l'INHA, Collection Jacques Doucet, fonds Poinssot, Archives 106, 130, 03, scans 073 to 075, 078, 080 and 082; reproduced for research and study). Each is straightened and set on its panel at the panel's own size: on the mihrab-side rail the panels are measured on Creswell's photograph, and on the maqsūra-side rail their widths and the uprights between them are measured on the restoration photographs. Black or white patches are breaks in the prints. With Rail photos off, the outside of the mihrab-side rail shows Creswell's photograph and the other three faces are plain panels.

The rail counts come from six photographs of the two new rails laid flat during the restoration (fonds Poinssot, scans 073 to 075, 078, 080 and 082), three for each face, joined into one image per face. Each rail was counted on both of its faces from a different set of photographs, and the two counts agree: 16 on the mihrab-side rail, which is also Creswell's count, and 18 on the maqsūra-side rail. The 18 are narrower, so both rails come out the same length. On the model the inside of the mihrab-side rail has the panels of its outside, and the 18 of the maqsūra-side rail are set on the same length of rail by their measured widths, good to about a fifth of a panel.
