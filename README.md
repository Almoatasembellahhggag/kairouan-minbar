# Kairouan Minbar Panels

An interactive comparison of the panels of the minbar of the Great Mosque of Kairouan, before and after the 1907–10 restoration. Panel numbers follow Henri Saladin (1899, fig. 52). Click a number to read what the sources say about that panel.

Notes marked "Identification" on the site are matches made by comparing photographs, not statements in the sources. Check the sources before citing.

Photographs: Saladin (1899), fig. 52 and Pl. XXI, XXVI, XXVII; Wikimedia Commons (détails du minbar; Un panneau sculpté du minbar); museum photographs and a catalogue page; Garrigues, Tunis, no. 271 (Wikimedia Commons); K. A. C. Creswell, *Early Muslim Architecture* II (1940), Pl. 89–90; Creswell prints in the Victoria and Albert Museum, 720-1929 to 726-1929. They are reproduced for research and study and are not covered by any licence on this repository.

## Editing

The main page shows the images, panels and notes. The Edit link opens `edit.html`, where you answer three questions for each panel: is the Saladin number correct, does it exist in Creswell (yes, no, or yes in a different position), and is the Creswell panel at that position new. Switch to Photos to assign each photograph to the panels it shows, including new panels marked N on Creswell's photograph. Edits stay in that browser until you press Save file and commit the result here as `edits.json`, which the site then loads for everyone.
